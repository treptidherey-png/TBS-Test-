# Research Priority

Use this order when developing a content idea:

1. Existing approved TBS thinking
2. Real-world evidence from TBS work and buyer conversations
3. Relevant market intelligence
4. Competitor content as mechanism/format reference
5. New research only for genuine gaps

Competitor content is never a source for TBS facts, stories, claims, or proof.

External facts require verification.

Never invent specifics, results, numbers, experiences, or quotes.
