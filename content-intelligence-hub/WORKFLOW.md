# MVP Workflow

## 1. CAPTURE

Capture anything worth remembering without forcing perfect classification.

Examples:

- content draft
- buyer objection
- founder observation
- useful external research
- market signal
- competitor mechanism
- proof/evidence
- performance learning

Everything enters the **Master Content Inventory** first.

## 2. MASTER CONTENT INVENTORY

The inventory is the reusable pool of TBS content and intelligence.

A record can move through:

`Captured → Developed → Recommended → Awaiting Trepti Approval → Approved`

Approved content remains available until it is deliberately selected for a calendar slot.

## 3. DEVELOP AN IDEA

When Trepti wants to post about a topic:

1. Search the Master Content Inventory first.
2. Separate what is already known from what is merely related.
3. Identify missing evidence or reasoning.
4. Research only the gaps.
5. Form one content direction.
6. Define one primary content job.
7. Run a repetition and evidence check.
8. Recommend one direction and explain rejected alternatives.

The system must prefer existing content over creating another piece when an existing piece can reasonably fill the need.

## 4. APPROVAL GATE

The system stops at:

**Awaiting Trepti Approval**

No drafting, publishing, outreach, or execution happens before approval.

Only an approved item can be moved into the calendar.

## 5. MOVE TO CALENDAR

When an approved piece is selected for a specific publishing slot:

`Approved → Scheduled`

The item is removed from the available inventory view and appears in the Content Calendar with its date and slot.

If it is removed from the calendar before publication:

`Scheduled → Approved`

It returns to the reusable inventory rather than becoming a duplicate draft.

## 6. PUBLISHING AND LEARNING

Future layers can extend the same lifecycle:

`Scheduled → Published → Learning`

Publishing and measurement remain outside this MVP until the decision workflow is proven.
